## 1. Product details

**Name:** Relient — Custom Software & AI Automation

**What it is:** We build software around how a business already works: CRMs, client portals, dashboards and internal tools. AI agents and automation are built in, so repetitive work happens on its own. The business ends up with one system instead of Excel, WhatsApp and five different apps, and owns all of it.

**Problems it solves**
- Work, leads and customer data are spread across Excel, WhatsApp, email and several apps.
- Follow-ups and reminders depend on someone remembering.
- Staff copy-paste the same data between tools.
- The owner can't see what's happening without asking someone.
- Off-the-shelf software charges per user every month and doesn't fit how the team works.

**What we build**
- **Custom software:** CRMs, client portals, dashboards, internal tools
- **AI agents:** reply to and qualify new enquiries, answer from company documents, draft reports and updates
- **Automation:** connect forms, CRM, WhatsApp, email and sheets; automatic reminders and lead routing

**What every client gets**
- A system designed around their process
- Their existing Excel data moved in
- Working software to review every week
- Full ownership of the software and data, with no per-user monthly fees and no lock-in
- Support after launch

**Proof (case studies)**
1. **Amacs India (real estate, Mysuru):** they were paying every month for LeadRat CRM. We built them a one-time custom CRM covering leads, property management and employee attendance, with a camera photo captured at every login and logout. They own it and pay no monthly subscription. *Delivered.*
2. **Agencies:** client portal where clients see timelines, deliverables and updates in one place, instead of agencies juggling Trello, Drive, ClickUp, email and calls. *In build, early access.*

**Who decides:** Owner / MD / Director / Operations Head

## 3. FAQs for the telecaller (incl. objections)

**Q: What does Relient do?**
"We build custom software for businesses: things like CRMs, dashboards and client portals, with automation built in. It replaces Excel, WhatsApp and the tools that don't fit how you work."

**Q: How much does it cost?**
"It depends on what your business needs. That's why the first call is free: our founder understands your setup and then gives you a fixed quote. There are no per-user monthly charges."
*(Never quote a number.)*

**Q: We already use software / Excel / WhatsApp.**
"That's exactly who we work with. Most businesses we speak to use a mix of Excel, WhatsApp and a few apps. On the call we'll look at where things slip through and what one system would look like. Your existing data comes along with you."

**Q: What kind of businesses do you work with?**
"Any business with a team that manages customers, leads or operations. For example, we built a custom CRM for Amacs India, a real estate company in Mysuru, and a client portal for agencies."

**Q: Is it ready-made software?**
"No, it's built around your process. That's the difference."

**Q: What is the AI part?**
"Things like replying to new enquiries instantly, sending reminders automatically, and drafting reports, so your team spends less time on repetitive work."

**Q: How long does it take?**
"A first working version is usually ready within a few weeks. The exact timeline is confirmed on the call."

**Q: Send me details on WhatsApp.**
"Sure, I'll send it right now. Can I also block a 20-minute slot with our founder to walk you through it? Tomorrow or day after?"
*(Always try for a slot. If they refuse, send the brochure and mark it as a follow-up.)*

**Q: Not interested / busy.**
"No problem. One quick question: how does your team track customers and follow-ups today?"
*(If they answer, there's usually a pain point. Offer the call again. If not, thank them and close.)*

**Q: Do we own the software?**
"Yes, you own the software and all your data."

**Q: Who will we talk to?**
"Bhargav, our founder, personally."

## 4. Calling script

**Opening**
"Hello, am I speaking with [Name]? Hi sir/ma'am, this is [Caller] from Relient Solutions, Hyderabad. Do you have one minute?"

**Context**
"We build custom software and automation for businesses. Basically, we replace Excel, WhatsApp and scattered tools with one system built around how your team works."

**Pain question (most important; let them talk)**
"Can I ask how your team manages customers, leads and follow-ups today? Excel, WhatsApp, or some software?"
→ *Listen. Note their answer in remarks.*

**Bridge**
- If Excel/WhatsApp: "Most businesses we speak to have the same setup. The common problems are follow-ups getting missed, the same data being entered twice, and the owner not having a clear view."
- If they use software: "Does it fit how your team works, or are people still doing some things in Excel or WhatsApp on the side?"

**Proof (one line)**
"For example, Amacs India in Mysuru was paying every month for a CRM. We built them their own CRM with leads, property management and staff attendance in one place, and there's no monthly fee."

**Qualify (capture in remarks)**
1. What does your business do?
2. How many people are in your team?
3. What's the biggest thing that slips through today?
4. Who decides on software, you or someone else?

**Ask for the meeting**
"Our founder Bhargav does a free 20-minute call to look at your current setup and show you what one system would look like for you. There's no obligation. Would tomorrow at [time] or [day] at [time] work better?"

**Confirm**
"Great. I've booked [day, time]. I'll send the details on WhatsApp to this number. Is that okay?"

**Close**
"Thank you, sir/ma'am. Have a good day."
